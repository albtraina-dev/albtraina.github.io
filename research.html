Research

Understanding the Universe through galaxies and cosmic evolution

Research interests

My research focuses on understanding how galaxies form, evolve, and interact with their environment across cosmic time.

Main topics

Galaxy Evolution

Studying the physical processes that regulate galaxy growth, star formation, and transformations over billions of years.

Observational Cosmology

Using astronomical observations to investigate the connection between galaxies and the large-scale structure of the Universe.
