Publications

Selected works on galaxies, dust, AGN, and cosmic evolution

Research through publications

My research investigates the evolution of galaxies and active galactic nuclei across cosmic time, with a particular focus on dust-obscured star formation, supermassive black hole growth, and the role of environment in shaping galaxy evolution.

Below are a few selected publications highlighting some of the main topics I have worked on in recent years.

Featured publications

STAR FORMATION · GALAXY EVOLUTION

The observed total star formation rate function up to z ∼ 6: Complementary UV and IR contributions and comparison with state-of-the-art galaxy formation models

Traina, A., Gruppioni, C., Delvecchio, I., et al.

Combining ultraviolet and infrared observations to characterize the total star formation activity of galaxies across cosmic time and compare observations with state-of-the-art galaxy formation models.

DUST · ALMA · GALAXY EVOLUTION

A3COSMOS: Dust mass function and dust mass density at 0.5 < z < 6

Traina, A., Magnelli, B., Gruppioni, C., et al.

Characterizing the evolution of the dust mass function and cosmic dust density from the local Universe to high redshift using ALMA observations.

DUST · STAR FORMATION · ALMA

A3COSMOS: The infrared luminosity function and dust-obscured star formation rate density at 0.5 < z < 6

Traina, A., Gruppioni, C., Delvecchio, I., et al.

Measuring the contribution of dust-obscured galaxies to the cosmic star formation history through the statistical exploitation of the A3COSMOS ALMA archive.

AGN · PROTOCLUSTERS · X-RAY

The properties of X-ray-selected active galactic nuclei in protoclusters pinpointed by enormous Lyman alpha nebulae

Traina, A., Vito, F., Arrigoni-Battaia, F., et al.

Investigating the AGN population in high-redshift protoclusters using deep X-ray observations and exploring the enhancement of supermassive black hole activity in dense environments.

PRIMA · DUST · FUTURE FACILITIES

Recovering the dust mass budget with PRIMA

Traina, A., Pozzi, F., Calura, F., et al.

Forecasting the capability of the future PRIMA mission to investigate the dust content of galaxies and recover the evolution of the cosmic dust mass budget.
