Scientific publications and selected research highlights

Research through publications

My research explores galaxy evolution, dust-obscured star formation, active galactic nuclei, and the growth of supermassive black holes across cosmic time.

Featured papers

2025

The observed total star formation rate function up to z ∼ 6: Complementary UV and IR contributions and comparison with state-of-the-art galaxy formation models

Traina, A., Gruppioni, C., Delvecchio, I., et al.

Combining ultraviolet and infrared observations to characterize the total star formation activity of galaxies across cosmic time and compare observations with state-of-the-art galaxy formation models.

Research highlight: Reconstructing the total star formation history of the Universe up to z ∼ 6 by accounting for both unobscured and dust-obscured star formation.

Read paper ADS
2025

The properties of X-ray-selected active galactic nuclei in protoclusters pinpointed by enormous Lyman alpha nebulae

Traina, A., Vito, F., Arrigoni-Battaia, F., et al.

Investigating the AGN population in high-redshift protoclusters using deep X-ray observations.

Research highlight: AGN show a larger overdensity than SMGs in protoclusters, pointing towards enhanced supermassive black hole growth in dense environments.

Read paper ADS
