2025

The properties of X-ray-selected active galactic nuclei in protoclusters

Traina, A., et al.

Investigating the AGN population in high-redshift protoclusters using deep X-ray observations, with a focus on the connection between dense environments and the growth of supermassive black holes.

Research highlight: AGN show a larger overdensity than dust-obscured star-forming galaxies in protoclusters, providing evidence for enhanced black hole growth in dense environments.

Read paper ADS
