About me

Hi! I'm Alberto Traina, an astrophysicist and postdoctoral associate at the MIT Kavli Institute for Astrophysics and Space Research.

My research mainly focuses on the characterization of the properties of galaxies and the co-evolution with their supermassive black holes. I study the evolution of galaxies and AGN at different redshifts, from the late to the early Universe, and in different environments (field, protoclusters) using a multiwavelength approach that involves the analysis of data from the millimeter to the X-ray spectral regimes.
